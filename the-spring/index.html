Life in Springfield

Main street has been here a long time

The Spring sits on Central Street in the heart of downtown. Fire insurance mapmakers drew this town block by block more than a century ago. New photos of the building are coming as finishes land; until then, here is downtown on paper.

Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps, Sanborn Map Company. Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division, via Wikimedia Commons. Public domain.

Five homes over the street

Each residence has its own furnace, air, water heater, and laundry. Quartz in the kitchen. Appliances included. Gas and electric are separately metered; water is shared and handled fairly in the lease.

Downstairs is still becoming something

There are already one or two street-facing entries on Central. We’re not rushing to name the shop, studio, or office that belongs there. For Springfield, that open ground floor is part of what makes the building interesting, living upstairs, and a chance for something local at the sidewalk when the timing is right.
