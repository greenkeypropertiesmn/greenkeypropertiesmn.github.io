The building

Brick, glass, and main street

The Spring holds the corner of daily life downtown: storefront glass at the sidewalk, a private foyer and stair for the residences, and five homes above.

Life in Springfield

Main street has been here a long time

The Spring sits on Central Street in the heart of downtown. Fire insurance mapmakers drew this town block by block more than a century ago. Here is downtown on paper.

Second floor

Five homes over the street

Each residence has its own furnace, air, water heater, and laundry. Granite in the kitchen. Appliances included. Gas and electric are separately metered; water is shared and handled fairly in the lease.
